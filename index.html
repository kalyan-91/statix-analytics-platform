About STATIX

What is STATIX?

STATIX is a simple and interactive platform that helps you understand your own data. There's no fixed template underneath — every chart, statistic, and insight is calculated live from whatever file you upload.

Upload any dataset

CSV or Excel, any columns, any shape.

Clean your data

Handle missing values, duplicates, and types.

Calculate statistics

Numeric, categorical and date summaries.

Discover insights

Patterns generated from your actual numbers.

Vision

Make data analysis simple, visual, attractive, and accessible to everyone — the dataset controls the application, not the other way around.
